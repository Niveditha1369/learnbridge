Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

LearnBridge collects personal information necessary to facilitate tutoring connections, including your full name, email address, city, teaching credentials, subject specializations, and requested tuition parameters. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and are never stored in plaintext.

2. How Information is Displayed

Public tutor profiles display your name, city, headline, bio, hourly rate, and subjects taught. Your private email address and confidential contact information are strictly protected and never displayed on public search pages.

3. Tuition Inquiries and Communication

When a student submits a tuition request or a tutor responds to an open requirement, information is transmitted securely within the platform. Both parties can review academic requirements, schedule preferences, and agreed rates inside their dashboards.

4. Security & Cookies

Our web service utilizes HTTPS encryption, secure session cookies (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax), and server-side CSRF validation to protect your account against unauthorized requests.

5. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

For inquiries about your stored data or to request account deletion, please contact privacy@learnbridge.com.
